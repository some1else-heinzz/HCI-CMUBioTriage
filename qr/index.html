Snakebite quick guide

Call for help (Philippines)

Emergency: 911. Philippine Red Cross: 143.

Get medical care now. Do not wait for symptoms.

First aid

Move away from the snake. Keep the person and bitten limb still. Remove rings and tight items.

Do not cut the bite, suck venom, or use a tourniquet.

Handoff summary

Tell responders: location or landmark, bite time, body part, symptoms, and first aid given.
